# The Design Philosophy

## its not a tutorial app!

I decided to make the app a sort of self-assessment tool or training augment, rather than a tutorial app. After all, there's plenty of in-person coaching lessons available in most cities and free online YouTube tutorial videos (which is how I got started).

Instead, I wanted to do something different which hadn't really been done before, and can't currently be achieved via the two methods I just mentioned.

## qualities

The app was built with the following in mind: 

- **Traceur-friendly**: I designed this with the thought of whether I myself would enjoy using it

- **Keep It Simple**: Simple and intuitive to use, no unnecessarily complex functionality

-  **Quality over Quantity**: I didn’t want to make the app’s focus about how many moves I could or how "good" it was on a basic scale of 1-10

- **Safe and Secure**: Fully offline and client-only so your data doesn’t go anywhere and it’s fully yours

- **Open Source**: If you want to suggest improvements or fork and extend the app, feel free to do so!

## rating system


### control

I define _control_ as how well you would handle a movement:
- under normal circumstances 
- after adding distance/height and speed!
- understanding common bad habits and also safe bail techniques 

### competency 
Furthermore, the app uses a Bad, Ok, Good assessment system alongside colours to quickly show information. As traceurs we generally understand our own bodies best as to why we are good and bad at certain things.  

### areas of concern

Furthermore, the apps focus are on core fundamental moves and will not include niche/hyper advanced moves ie. triple-kongs, as a strong foundation in the fundamentals means that you are on track to doing well on those too!
