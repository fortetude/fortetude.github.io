# Autogenerating a Line

Subject to Change! 🛠️
{: .label .label-yellow}

- Tap on the sparkle icon (✨) on the top-left
- The drawer will open displaying the following menu
    - ![autogenerate](/assets/images/sandbox-autogenerate.png)
- Choose a range from the slider and click **Generate Line**!
- Confirm if overwriting or appending the Sandbox.
    - ![autogenerate-confirm](/assets/images/sandbox-autogenerate-confirm.png)
