# Changing Sandbox Items
How to reorganise and tweak your Sandbox Line quickly on the fly  

## Reordering List Items
Simply drag and drop using the grab bars (☰) to reposition items.
![sandbox-reorder](/assets/images/sandbox-reorder.gif)


## Swapping Sides
Swipe left to change the direction of a move, no changes if it has no specific direction. 
![sandbox-swap](/assets/images/sandbox-swap.gif)
> The side the colors are on and the alignment of the text hints at which side the move is on.


## Deleting Items
Swipe right to quickly remove an item from the Sandbox.
![sandbox-delete](/assets/images/sandbox-delete.gif)
