# Adding Lines to Sandbox

  
- Select a Line by tapping on its tile.
  <details>  
  <summary>Example</summary>  
  <img src ="/assets/images/lines-taptap.gif">
  </details>  
- Tap on the road icon (**\|┆\|+**) near the top-right to add to sandbox
- A confirmation dialog will allow you to 
   - Go back (tap outside of the dialog box)
   - **Append** to sandbox
   - **Overwrite** the sandbox.
- ![sandbox](/assets/images/lines-sandbox.png)
