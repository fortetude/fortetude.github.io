# Moves Screen

This section is where you can self-assess your stability and progress your moves.

--- 

{: .note}
> On first install, all the Moves will display as white, this is normal and each tile will gain color once you [edit and save](/pages/user-guide/moves/editing_moves) them for the first time.



| fresh install                                    | after use                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| ![fresh-install](/assets/images/moves-white.png) | ![after-use](/assets/images/moves-colored.png) | 

- The color represents the **Competency** of the move.
- The three icons (and ticks) represent whether an **Area of Concern** is in play for the move.
- The colored bar at the bottom of each tile represents the **Control** of the move.

> To understand what all this means, head to the [rating](/pages/design#rating-system) section.
